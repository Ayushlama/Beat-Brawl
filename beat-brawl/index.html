Beat Brawl

Two stickmen fight to whatever is playing. Bass throws punches, snares throw kicks, hi-hats make the other one dodge. Load a song and the fight is paced to finish exactly when it ends.

Press play on the demo beat, or load a song from your device. The fight starts when the sound does.

Songs are decoded in your browser and never uploaded. MP3, WAV, OGG and M4A work. With a loaded song, the health bars are paced to the song length: nobody can be knocked out early, and the loser goes down when the music ends. The demo beat loops, so it plays in rounds instead. Landing hits fills the energy bar. When it is full, the next snare fires a Blast Wave that cannot be dodged. Lower the sensitivity if the fight looks frantic.
